# HomsScanner — What it can do

English · [العربية](INSTRUCTIONS.ar.md)

HomsScanner lets you scan paper documents and get a ready-made PDF, without extra software. It runs quietly on your PC and opens as a web page.

![Scan page](docs/images/01-scan-page.png)

## Features

- **Finds your scanner by itself** — USB or network. If it is not found, type its IP address in *Scan settings*. *Check available devices* shows what was found.
  ![Device check](docs/images/03-device-check.png)
- **Scan settings** — quality (100–600 dpi), colour / grayscale / black & white, flatbed glass or automatic document feeder, and **both sides** of the paper.
  ![Scan settings](docs/images/02-scan-settings.png)
- **Pages appear as they are scanned** — add more pages any time.
- **Edit your pages** — select pages, rotate them, delete one page (with **Undo**), and **drag pages to change their order**.
  ![Pages](docs/images/04-pages.png)
  ![Undo](docs/images/05-undo.png)
- **Full-screen viewer** — open any page, zoom in, move around, and go to the next/previous page.
  ![Viewer](docs/images/06-viewer.png)
- **Make the PDF** — choose a file name, then download it or save it to the desktop.
  ![Name dialog](docs/images/07-name-dialog.png)
  ![PDF ready](docs/images/08-pdf-ready.png)
- **Works inside other programs** — your organisation's app can show the scanner inside its own page and receive the PDF directly (*Save & attach*).
  ![Embedded](docs/images/09-embedded.png)
- **English and Arabic** — the screen switches language and reading direction (left-to-right / right-to-left) automatically. Light and dark themes are supported.
- **Starts with Windows** and lives in the system tray (next to the clock). Right-click the icon for *Settings*.
  ![Settings](docs/images/10-settings.png)
- **Private** — your scans stay on your computer; the service only answers to your own PC and to websites you allow.
